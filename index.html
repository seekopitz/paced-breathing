How to breathe

One breath, four parts, on a loop — follow the circle, or close your eyes and follow the tone.

Inhale
Draw air in steadily through your nose, still filling as the circle reaches its widest.
Hold
Keep the air in with your throat open and your shoulders down — holding is not bracing.
Exhale
Let it out slowly and evenly, emptying further than feels automatic.
Rest
Stay empty and wait out the count before the next inhale — the part most people skip.

A 0 in a pattern removes that phase. If you feel lightheaded, shorten the counts.
